## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Hello Health brings every part of your wellbeing into one calm, beautiful place — and keeps it
free to use.

**One dashboard for your whole day**
See steps, distance, calories, heart rate, sleep, and your daily readiness at a glance. Hello Health
reads from Android Health Connect, so data from your watch and other apps flows in automatically.

**Activity & workouts**
Track your activity rings, build workout routines, and log live training sessions with a rest timer
that keeps running even when you leave the app.

**Nutrition made simple**
Log meals by searching a huge food database or scanning a barcode. Track macros, fiber, and your
own custom goals.

**Mood & mind**
Log how you feel in a tap, or use the on-device face-scan to estimate your mood — the image never
leaves your phone. Watch your mood timeline and see how rest, activity, and nutrition connect.

**Sleep & recovery**
Understand your sleep stages, recovery, and how your nights shape your readiness each morning.

**AI coaching**
Ask your personal coach for guidance grounded in your own data. Your API usage is private and
server-side — no keys live on your device.

**Insights that join the dots**
Cross-dimension insights surface what actually moves your health: how sleep affects activity, how
nutrition tracks against your goals, and where to focus next.

**Your data, your control**
Your health data syncs securely to your account, protected per-user so only you can read it. Revoke
any permission anytime, and delete your account and all its data permanently whenever you choose.

Hello Health is free to use. Start today and make sense of your health.
